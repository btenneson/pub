From the Boring Game to the Last Gift

A Dependency-Governed Braid of Sovereign Gifts

This web note presents the new structural existence theorem from Section 13 and Appendix A.37 of the 42-page revised manuscript. It is a theorem excerpt, not the complete manuscript. The PDF and LaTeX source are pending upload.

Definition 13.1 — Structural sovereign gift-protocol class

Let C+ be the class of GBG+ presentations. Let CS be the class of sovereign gift-protocol presentations satisfying Definitions 3.1 and 7.1 and the cumulative admission and STOP requirements of Section 6, with one distinguished board-player S. The atom supply and effective grammar are parameters of each presentation. A completed playable witness contains at least one executed legal move. The explicit moving-completion convention of Proposition 6.4 is permitted. Write Constr(C) for C ≠ ∅.

Theorem 13.2 — Board-created sovereign gift model

There exists a finite GBG+ presentation whose board-player constructs, by announced and declared legal transitions, a finite member of CS with exactly one admitted gift, guarded B8 (abdication), and an actual legal sovereign move.

Constr(C+) ⇒ Constr(CS).

Proof (condensed from Appendix A.37)

1. Constructing GBG+. Choose a stationary underlying ordinary GBG with ordinary participants p1, p2. Its enlarged game has unique board-player p0, label map F(r)=max(0,r−1) on {0,1,2}, and complete states c0, …, c6. Declare exactly the following singleton-domain partial unary transitions, each authorized solely to p0.

TransitionDeclared operation
c0 → c1Announce target role assignment.
c1 → c2Create target roles S=p1 and P1′=p2.
c2 → c3Announce protocol and instance grammar installation.
c3 → c4Install dormant target and candidate contract.
c4 → c5Announce admission, contingent on the finite witness.
c5 → c6Record the successful witness check, admit B8, return target specification.

The last transition is defined for this fixed verified instance, not as a general consistency decider. The stationary underlying game, finite record, fixed authorization, terminal status at c6, and label map satisfy the five GBG+ axioms and the declared mutable-board requirements.

2. Target roles and dormancy. The target participants are S and P1′ with target labels 0 and 1 and their own label map. The original p0 is not a target participant. The dormant G1 and zero-gift activation H1 have no legal moves. The initial discretionary privilege bit is 1. Administrative moves occur outside the dormant game.

3. Typed gift. Choose A={B8} and the grammar offering only g1=B8 at index one. The dormant G2 stores B8 but has no enabled internal move. Its activation H2 has two states u0,u1 with privilege values 1 and 0, and the sole partial rule u0 → u1 authorized to S. There is no reverse edge. The versioned embedding preserves the participant identities, initial privilege, and fixed external logic, with the old dormant record retained. Execution requires activation at u0 and yields u1. The fixed observation of authorization to abdicate changes from O(H1)=0 to O(H2)=1. The gift has exactly one distinct atom.

4. Cumulative consistency. Use a fixed classical first-order metalanguage with finite stage, state, and participant sorts. Let T0 describe G1 and H1; let Δ1 describe the additional records, privileges, authorization, and observations.

RecordStatesRegistryLegal internal edges
G1{d0}∅∅
H1{u0}∅∅
G2{d0}{B8}∅
H2{u0,u1}{B8}{(u0,S,u1)}

All omitted edges are absent; b(d0)=b(u0)=1 and b(u1)=0. These finite tables and their complements satisfy the stage-scoped descriptions together. By soundness, Con(T0) and Con(T0 ∪ Δ1) hold. The explicit witness discharges the consistency gate; S1 admits the gift.

5. STOP and execution. A second gift requires two distinct atoms, impossible in the chosen one-atom grammar. S3 therefore justifies STOP at G2. S2 and S4 have no triggering candidate in this instance. Adopt Proposition 6.4's explicit completion convention, activate H2, and execute its sole legal move u0 → u1 as S. The completed witness has one actual legal move and one sovereign.

6. XYZ representation and conclusion. Encode the complete states by distinct finite strings over a nonempty alphabet. Each declared operation is a partial unary rule. The constructor's outer rules remain fixed while the target registry changes. The target satisfies the stipulated gift grammar, typed admission, cumulative consistency, nonredundancy, STOP, and movement requirements. Hence CS is nonempty, establishing the stated implication. □

Exact scope

This is a structural model-existence theorem for the explicitly defined gift-protocol class. It neither asserts realizability of every candidate gift nor a section/projection identity for all GBG+ models. Unrestricted syntactic relative consistency remains open. No independent machine-checker receipt or peer-review certification is claimed.
