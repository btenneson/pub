Record of the accepted program
Checkpoint ID: DNI-PHYS-CP-2026-10-20-01. Status: source architecture accepted;
source theories and interface not yet independently verified. Work now authorized:
audit and mathematical repairs of the two source theories, not unannounced experiments
or a commitment to a particular physical interpretation.

D: relativity side
The proposed deterministic-side source theory is a provenance-preserving multiarti-
cle of Einstein’s original early-20th-century special- and general-relativity works. Initial
core source register:
  • D-SR-1905: Albert Einstein, Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper (1905), Annalen
    der Physik 17, 891–921. Primary special-relativity development.
  • D-ME-1905: Albert Einstein, Ist die Trägheit eines Körpers von seinem Energiein-
    halt abhängig? (1905), Annalen der Physik 18, 639–641. Optional but relevant
    energy-inertia consequence.
  • D-GR-1915: Albert Einstein, Die Feldgleichungen der Gravitation (25 November
    1915), Sitzungsberichte der Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, 844–847.
    Final field equations.
  • D-GR-1916: Albert Einstein, Die Grundlage der allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie
    (1916), Annalen der Physik 49, 769–822. Extended exposition of general relativity.
Source selection policy: retain Einstein’s historical text and every historical assump-
tion with its citation; do not silently treat earlier superseded field equations as simulta-
neous axioms. Translation, signature, units, gauge and time-domain differences must
be logged. Distinguish Einstein’s original claims from later rigorous Cauchy-problem
theorems. Do not claim global determinism absent appropriate data, gauge and global-
hyperbolicity hypotheses.

N: quantum side
The accepted source is Asher Peres, Quantum Theory: Concepts and Methods
(Kluwer, 1993), ISBN 978-0-7923-2549-9, DOI 10.1007/0-306-47120-5. N is a source-
traceable probabilistic operational quantum theory, not a declaration that all
quantum evolution or all ontologies are indeterministic.

The intended mathematical vocabulary includes complex Hilbert spaces, density
operators, deterministic unitary evolution of isolated systems, specified quantum
tests/measurements, outcome probabilities and state updates. More general completely
positive quantum instruments may be added only as a separately tagged modern oper-
ational extension unless verified against the Peres source. The source is one book: its
internal chapters may be audited as modules; this is not an independent multiarticle zip
of multiple papers.

The mathematical DNI interface: reserved, not constructed
After separate source audits, possible mathematical relations may include typed trans-
lations, jointly interpretable structures, observation/readout maps, finite-history proba-
bilistic comparisons, proof transformations, and incompatibility theorems. No Einstein–
Peres bridge axioms are approved in this checkpoint. Neither theory may be altered
merely to obtain compatibility. No quantum-gravity or determinism-versus-reality conclu-
sion is presupposed.

Provenance and verification contract
For every record retain (source, section/page, version/translation, formal lan-
guage, hypotheses, statement, proof/dependencies, checker, status). Separate
historical source assertion, mathematical restatement, derived on-paper result,
machine-verified result, conjecture, and repair proposal. Formal transport requires
a declared proof-preserving translation; mere thematic resemblance is insufficient. No
claim of full deductive closure or independent certification without receipts.

Audits now authorized
  1. D: build primary-source register; compare and normalize notational variants without
     conflating historical theories; test special-relativistic flat sector, field equations and
     determinism assumptions; issue repairs and proof obligations.
  2. N: map the authorized Peres book’s sections; isolate source-level axioms from mod-
     ern additions; check normalization, dynamics and conditional measurement calcu-
     lus; issue repairs and proof obligations.
  3. Keep D and N audit outputs separate; defer the actual DNI interface and all experi-
     mental runs until specifically authorized.
