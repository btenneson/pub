\begin{abstract}
We extend the integrated singleton theory of ATP dreaming to \emph{one federation containing five automated theorem provers}. The fixed federation count is $m=1$ and its membership count is $n=5$; each member $M_i$ may run $r_i\ge2$ internal GBG+ processes, including Knowledge and Awareness, with its own uniquely distinguished local board-player. A federation-level controller, if present, is a separate authorized orchestration interface, not a sixth ATP or a substitute for local verification. We develop typed local and federated checkpoints, relative and global deductive surplus, cross-member historical awareness, authenticated messages, noninterference, fair service, and fully charged costs. We prove local closure conservation without imported object premises and global closure conservation when all admitted object theorems are certified from a fixed \emph{shared} base; we show why a member's closure may expand under authorized imports without contradicting global conservativity. We construct genuine five-member Gestalt witnesses, distinguish their logical value from their costed search value, establish conditional structural reduction to five singleton games, and exhibit a finite five-member model with positive local and cross-member certified deposits. We identify what changes when the five AMLD specialties are assigned to the five ATPs, what remains unimplemented at the AMLD+0 settlement-protocol level, and which general claims remain open. We further give a typed, authorized reading-and-dreaming extension, prove finite certified reading and proof-obligation identification results, construct arbitrarily long and infinite staged self-directed study executions, and separate conservative dream deposits from genuine textbook-driven theory extension. These are existence proofs for a specified AMLD+0-compatible extension, not evidence that the unmodified settler already possesses an autonomous reading interface. The article integrates and extends, but does not overwrite, four source manuscripts and the prior singleton synthesis. Version 0.4R makes explicit the limits of missing-subgoal detection, the local cost tariff, and target freezing across authorized basis changes.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, vocabulary, and roadmap}
\begin{definition}[Three independent counts]
There is exactly $m=1$ federation $\mathfrak F=\{M_1,M_2,M_3,M_4,M_5\}$, and exactly $n=5$ ATP members in it. ATP $M_i$ hosts $r_i\ge2$ ordinary internal dreaming processes $D_{i,1},\ldots,D_{i,r_i}$, whose first two are Knowledge and Awareness. Each local GBG+ game has its \emph{own} distinguished board-player $B_i^\star=P_{i,0}$; no board-player is counted among the five ATPs. A distinct federation orchestration rule $\Omega$ may choose which member gets service or which authorized message is transported.
\end{definition}
The original finite-$n$ dreaming existence statements used $n$ to count ordinary \emph{internal game players}. In this article their quantified index is $r_i$ (or $r$ in homogeneous cases), never the federation membership count $n=5$. Likewise, one AMLD+0 federation does not mean only one ATP. The five ATPs might implement distinct settlement specialties, but that is an additional role assignment, not a mathematical consequence of the number five.

The architecture has four levels: one frozen federation frame, five individually authenticated local checkpoints, local dream games, and an authorized intermember message/verification layer. We first identify exactly which singleton theorems survive unchanged, then handle proofs and awareness that cross member boundaries. Finally we examine cooperation, costs, schedules, settlement and open problems.

\section{Frozen evidence, object theories, and typed BANKs}
Fix a finitary logic $F$ with an extensive, monotone, idempotent consequence operator $\Cn_F$. Let the globally authorized frozen object base be $\Sigma_0=\Gamma_F\cup\bigcup_{i=1}^5 K_{i,0}$, where $K_{i,0}=\operatorname{form}(\KB_{i,0})$; a shared background theory $\Gamma_F$ is stated separately from each member's premises. If members use different calculi, the definitions below require a common sound translation into $F$; otherwise no cross-member closure equality should be asserted.
\begin{definition}[Federation frozen checkpoint]
At common logical epoch $t_0$, freeze\[S_F=(\mathrm{id}_F,\Gamma_F,\Auth_F,V_F,\Cost_F,\mathcal T,(S_{i,0})_{i=1}^5),\]with local components\[S_{i,0}=(F_i,K_{i,0},A_{i,0},H_{i,0},V_{K_i},V_{A_i},\Auth_i,\mathrm{Tag}_i).\] The $H_{i,0}$ are authenticated time-indexed histories, including resolvable proof dependencies. $\mathcal T$ fixes cross-member translations and trust policies. Frozen source records are immutable; each dream maintains writable BANK copies, append-only proposal/rejection traces, and a charged event ledger. ``Shared checkpoint'' requires a consistent snapshot protocol or authenticated per-member epochs, not the fiction of an instantaneous physical freeze.
\end{definition}
Every accepted packet stores type, theorem or claim, proof or historical witness, declared source theory, origin $i$, historical epoch, scope, verifier decision, and a dependency/provenance chain. A message from $M_i$ is evidence \emph{to be checked} at $M_j$, not an axiom to be blindly inserted. A purportedly valid signed receipt does not establish the mathematical soundness of its producer's checker; signatures, authority and logical correctness are distinct obligations.
\begin{definition}[Three separate knowledge quantities]
Write $K_{i,j}$ for member $i$'s accepted object formulas after federation turn $j$. Define its \emph{locally derived surplus} $\Delta^{\rm loc}_{i,j}$ to be new entries certified using only $K_{i,0}\cup\Gamma_i$; its \emph{imported surplus} $\Delta^{\rm imp}_{i,j}$ is accepted material whose proof requires other authorized member bases; and its \emph{global deposited surplus} is $\Delta^F_j=(\bigcup_i K_{i,j})\setminus\Sigma_0$. The global \emph{latent} consequence set is $\Cn_F(\Sigma_0)\setminus\Sigma_0$. Packet-level novelty and formula-level novelty must be reported separately: the same theorem checked by three members may yield three new provenance receipts but only one global formula.
\end{definition}
For historical awareness, distinguish $\mathrm{Held}_{i,t}(\varphi,u)$ (member $i$ possessed accessible accepted receipt $u$ for $\varphi$ at $t$), $\mathrm{Ent}_{i,t}(\varphi)$ (the frozen declared premises of $i$ entailed $\varphi$), and $\mathrm{Op}_{i,t}(v)$ (recorded operational interface/version $v$). Their different evidence requirements survive the move from one ATP to five. Additional claims $\mathrm{Received}_{j\leftarrow i,t}(p)$ and $\mathrm{Accepted}_{j,t}(p)$ record authenticated delivery and local validation; neither entails $\mathrm{Held}_{j,t_0}(p)$ at an earlier epoch.

\section{Legal dreaming: local rules, messages, and a global schedule}
A federation state $X_j=(S_F,(X_{i,j})_{i=1}^5,Q_j,L_j,\omega_j,\mathrm{phase}_j)$ includes five local copy states, a typed message queue $Q_j$, a global cost ledger $L_j$, and a federation-service schedule $\omega_j$. A legal federation turn chooses an enabled member via $\Omega$, permits only a locally authorized $B_i^\star$ selection and legal $D_{i,k}$ response, records any candidate and verifier decision, and optionally transmits a typed packet under $\Auth_F$. Import or federation-level acceptance requires the relevant receiving verifier or jointly declared checker. The special board-players only authorize scheduling; they cannot make an invalid proof valid.
\begin{definition}[Federated dreaming episode]
A finite nonempty legal path is an episode when at least one member carries out an authenticated evidence-dependent read, reconstruction, duplicate check, or candidate verification from a frozen local or permitted federated record. A \emph{cross-member dream} additionally includes an authorized message whose received evidence is actually used. Idle turns alone do not count. A dream can fail to create any deposit.
\end{definition}
\begin{proposition}[Frozen-copy noninterference]
If every dream write is confined to the five copy BANKs, authenticated message queue and ledgers, and promotion to the live originals is forbidden, no finite dreaming prefix changes any $S_{i,0}$ or live original \emph{by a dream write}.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Induct on the federation transition count. Each transition respects the common write restriction; message transport changes only copied state and ledgers. Independent live activity is not a dream write.\end{proof}
A promotion protocol can be appended, but its fresh authorization, consistency, checker and cost obligations are external to the theorem. Permission to read a record is not permission to publish or modify it.

\section{Conservativity: local, global, and imported knowledge}
\begin{theorem}[Local conservativity under strictly local admission]
If for a fixed $i$ all new object deposits are certified consequences of $K_{i,0}\cup\Gamma_i$ and the latter remains included in the local closure calculation, then $\Cn_F(K_{i,j}\cup\Gamma_i)=\Cn_F(K_{i,0}\cup\Gamma_i)$ for every finite $j$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}The gate gives $K_{i,0}\cup\Gamma_i\subseteq K_{i,j}\cup\Gamma_i\subseteq\Cn_F(K_{i,0}\cup\Gamma_i)$. Monotonicity and idempotence give equality of the closures.\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Federation-wide closure conservation]
Assume every object formula accepted anywhere in the federation is independently certified in a common calculus as a consequence of the frozen $\Sigma_0$ with no new independent premises; all original $\Sigma_0$ remains accessible to the shared theory. Then for every finite $j$,
\[\Cn_F\left(\Sigma_0\cup\bigcup_{i=1}^5K_{i,j}\right)=\Cn_F(\Sigma_0).\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}The verification rule places all accepted formulas in $\Cn_F(\Sigma_0)$; both inclusions follow as in the local theorem.\end{proof}
\begin{example}[A member learns a theorem without enlarging the federation's closure]
Let $K_{1,0}=\{p\}$, $K_{2,0}=\{p\to q\}$, with $q$ not in either member's local closure and with no extra premises. On authorized exchange and independent checking, member 1 deposits $q$. Its local deductive closure expands relative to $\{p\}$; $q$ was already in $\Cn_F(\Sigma_0)$. This is a genuine distributed knowledge gain, not a counterexample to global conservativity.
\end{example}
If the network admits externally acquired axioms, oracle facts, changed inference rules, or an unsound verifier, the global conservation hypothesis can fail. Such a system must label these as \emph{knowledge-base extensions}, not quietly describe them as conservative dreaming.
\begin{proposition}[History claims need not change object closure]
A member can receive and verify a previously absent claim $\mathrm{Held}_{k,t_0}(\varphi,u)$ concerning another member's frozen history, increasing its Awareness BANK while leaving all object BANK projections unchanged.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Take a frozen authenticated source receipt not yet represented in the receiver's Awareness BANK; validate and record its historical packet using $V_{A_i}$. No object admission is performed.\end{proof}

\section{Five-member historical awareness and reflection}
For recipient $i$, subject $k$, and historic epoch $s$, define the \emph{warranted-access} modality $\mathsf K_{i,t}(\psi)$ to mean that $\psi$ belongs to $i$'s declared admissible meta-domain at $t$ and has an accepted witness available in $i$'s authorized view. The well-typed cross-member expression $\mathsf K_{i,t}(\mathsf K_{k,s}\varphi)$ asserts that $i$ now has an accepted warrant that $k$ possessed the precisely defined warranted view at $s$. It requires an authenticated, scoped witness for the \emph{inner} historical assertion and another eligible checked witness for the \emph{outer} one. It is not generated merely by forwarding $k$'s present assertion.
\begin{proposition}[No automatic mutual awareness]
Even if $\mathsf K_{k,s}\varphi$ holds, it need not follow that $\mathsf K_{i,t}(\mathsf K_{k,s}\varphi)$ holds for any different $i$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Restrict $i$'s authorized view to exclude the relevant authenticated receipt or give it no accepted scoped meta-witness. The inner claim can hold for $k$ while the receiver's outer evidence gate fails.\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Conditional receipt-gated mutual awareness]
Suppose (a) $\mathsf K_{k,s}\varphi$ holds; (b) $\mathsf K_{k,s}\varphi$ is a well-typed eligible meta-claim for $i$ at $t$; (c) transport supplies a provenance-complete authorized historic witness; and (d) $i$'s meta-checker accepts that witness for the exact nested claim. Then $\mathsf K_{i,t}(\mathsf K_{k,s}\varphi)$ holds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Eligibility and the accepted witness are the two conjuncts of the definition of $\mathsf K_{i,t}$.\end{proof}
Thus five members permit up to $5^2=25$ \emph{ordered} subject/observer pairs at a chosen two-time schema, including five self-pairs; this is an indexing count, not 25 automatically certified awareness facts. Longer chains $\mathsf K_{i_1,t_1}\cdots\mathsf K_{i_d,t_d}\varphi$ require a fresh typed witness at each outer level. A finite history, a signed receipt, or repeated self-assertion gives no unrestricted ascent or machine consciousness.

\section{Gestalt becomes substantive: five-member dependency}
The singleton case cannot satisfy a cross-\emph{member} dependency criterion, because it has no proper nonempty member coalition. With five ATPs, the distinction between results obtainable by a coalition and results obtainable by its individuals is mathematically real.
\begin{definition}[Federation Gestalt relative to common background]
For a declared shared theory $\Gamma_F$ and accessible evidence sets $E_i$, let $g$ be \emph{cross-member dependent} if $g\in\Cn_F(\Gamma_F\cup\bigcup_{i=1}^5 E_i)$ but $g\notin\Cn_F(\Gamma_F\cup E_i)$ for every $i$. It is \emph{coalition-minimal} if, further, no proper subset of the five evidence sets suffices. Evidence labels, access rights, and cost restrictions are part of the experiment; purely syntactic entailment is not a measured speedup.
\end{definition}
\begin{theorem}[Genuine five-member Gestalt witness]
There exists a finite sound propositional Horn instance in which $g$ follows from the union of all five members' evidence but does not follow from the union of any proper subset.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Give $M_i$ only the atom $a_i$ and put $a_1\wedge a_2\wedge a_3\wedge a_4\wedge a_5\to g$ in the common background. Modus ponens (or a Horn forward-chaining step) derives $g$ from all five atoms. For any proper coalition omit at least one atom, make all its evidence atoms true, the omitted atom false, and $g$ false. The Horn implication holds while $g$ fails. This countermodel proves non-entailment from every proper coalition.\end{proof}
\begin{corollary}[A hierarchy of coalition dependence]
For each $k\in\{2,3,4,5\}$ there is a five-member model with a target requiring precisely some designated $k$ members (and no proper subcoalition thereof).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}Use a $k$-atom conjunction implying $g$ and ignore the other $5-k$ evidence sets.\end{proof}
A cross-member Gestalt theorem is about \emph{distributed premises}. It does not say a centralized ATP given all five sets cannot prove $g$; indeed the union itself is the baseline of the proof. A stronger measured claim about acceleration requires a fixed comparison baseline, matching evidence access and all charged costs. Internal BANK synergy may coexist inside each $M_i$, but it is not a second copy of the same Gestalt result.

\section{Existence of a five-ATP dreaming federation}
\begin{theorem}[Five-member existence with local and federated certified surplus]
For any fixed finite $r_i\ge2$, there exists a set-coded dreaming model of one federation of five ATPs with: immutable seeded local snapshots; distinct verifier-gated Knowledge/Awareness BANKs at every member; locally and globally nonstarving infinite scheduling; nonnegative charged operations; a finite prefix in which both primary BANKs of every member accept a new packet; and a further finite prefix in which a jointly dependent federation-level target is certified from all five members' evidence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}For every $i$, use a disjoint copy of the Horn seed $\{p_i,p_i\to q_i\}$, an authenticated historical receipt for $p_i$, and a verified version receipt. Give each member its own $B_i^\star$ and its $r_i$ internal ordinary players. For auxiliary players $k\ge3$ provision one explicitly typed seed and one accepted witness, as in the prior finite-player existence argument. All records, states, actions and checkers are finite-coded. Within member $i$, the first cycle deposits $q_i$, $\mathrm{Held}_{i,t_0}(p_i)$ and one valid auxiliary packet per extra player; a further Knowledge no-op and Awareness turn deposit $\mathrm{VerifierVersion}_{i,t_0}(v_i)$. Hence each member has a locally productive prefix of $r_i+2$ internal service turns. Add a federated frozen evidence atom $a_i$ for each member, a common Horn rule $\bigwedge_{i=1}^5a_i\to g$, and authorized transports to one designated federation target checker. After receiving all five authenticated $a_i$ and checking the rule, deposit $g$. Give all members and players legal charged no-ops. Schedule federation members cyclically and their respective local players cyclically, extending the productive finite prefix indefinitely. Each member and each internal player is served infinitely often. No original checkpoint is written. The finite rule and witness encodings establish existence within the stipulated model.\end{proof}
This is an \emph{existence of some designed instance}, not a theorem that any actual five-ATP system will dream, jointly produce a new theorem, or finish a task. To qualify the construction as an execution of AMLD+0 proper, the federation must additionally implement its frozen conjecture/target, outcomes, verification gates, arbitration, settlement transcript and budget discipline. Merely having five ATPs and one controller is not an AMLD+0 terminal certificate.

\section{Five settlement specialties and the meaning of AMLD+0}
The familiar five labels $P,R,D,I,C$ may be assigned, \emph{as an explicit engineering hypothesis}, to $M_1,\ldots,M_5$. They stand for proof, refutation, dependence, independence and contradiction investigation, with $U$ an unresolved/unknown outcome rather than a sixth member. A different physical mapping or multiple specialties per member is possible if authorized. The distinct notions must not be collapsed:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item A member $M_i$ is an ATP host with its own Knowledge/Awareness dream processes.
\item A specialty $P,R,D,I,C$ is a role/interface and burden of proof, not automatically a member identity.
\item $B_i^\star$ is a local GBG+ board-player, not the AMLD+0 federation arbitrator.
\item $\Omega$ is the federation's authorized scheduling/arbitration policy; any Player-0 controller arrangement requires its own declared interface.
\item The BANK verifier checks packets; the terminal settlement verifier checks a complete declared conjecture settlement. Passing one does not substitute for the other.
\end{enumerate}
\begin{proposition}[Conditional compatibility with a five-specialty AMLD+0 specification]
If each assigned member implements the corresponding specialty interface, all intermember reads/handoffs obey a frozen federation authorization policy, settlement outcomes have an independent accepting checker, and the global scheduler/ledger satisfies the fixed AMLD+0 obligations, then the five-member dreaming system can be presented as an \emph{auxiliary dreaming layer} of that AMLD+0 execution.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Each local dream action and intermember message has an authorized interface image in the declared settlement trace, while terminal acceptance is decided solely by the settlement verifier. The hypotheses establish compositional compatibility; they do not prove any particular terminal claim.\end{proof}
In particular there is no general theorem that dreaming proves more conjectures, accelerates all specialties, guarantees any one of the six outcomes, or produces independently certified settled results. A dream-generated proof candidate still needs a correct checked proof of its target; historical Awareness cannot make an invalid object proof true.

\section{Fairness across five ATPs and their inner players}
\begin{theorem}[Nested nonstarvation under persistent enabledness]
Suppose each member remains enabled, each local internal player remains enabled (at least by a legal no-op), $\Omega$ selects members $M_{1+(j\bmod5)}$, and member $i$ selects its players cyclically on its successive service occasions. Then every pair $(i,k)$ receives infinitely many legal service opportunities.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Member $i$ occurs at turns $j\equiv i-1\pmod5$ infinitely often; on that infinite subsequence its $k$th cyclic internal selection occurs once per $r_i$ services, hence infinitely often.\end{proof}
For equal $r_i=r$, the deterministic nested cyclic policy serves each ordered member/player pair once every $5r$ \emph{federation-service turns}. This is an index-count bound under this exact policy, not a wall-clock bound; if actual service durations or physical gaps are unbounded, no physical-time deadline follows. General weak fairness with transient disabling also needs careful definitions; no finite observed prefix proves an infinite fairness property.

\section{Charges, cooperation, and no-free-speedup limits}
Total accountable cost should split into local scheduler/retrieval/search/proof checking/historical validation/write charges, federation arbitration, secure serialization/transport, receiving verification, buffering/queuing and (when desired) elapsed-time opportunity cost. For any finite dream history $\pi$ set
\[ C_F(\pi)=\sum_{i=1}^5 C_i^{\rm local}(\pi)+C_{\Omega}(\pi)+C_{\rm transport}(\pi)+C_{\rm receive}(\pi)+C_{\rm shared\ verify}(\pi).\]
Use a \emph{vector} of typed values rather than asserting object theorems, historical receipts and reflection claims have equal worth:
\[ Q(\pi)=\big(v_{K_1},v_{A_1},\ldots,v_{K_5},v_{A_5},v_G,-C_F\big)_\pi.\]
The finite Pareto-undominated-history theorem from the singleton case still applies to any nonempty finite feasible set, but says nothing about uniquely optimal policies or whether the Pareto frontier can be found cheaply.
\begin{theorem}[Five-member no-free-speedup bound, conditional]
Let $\mathcal H_F$ be the successful cooperative histories and $\mathcal H_B$ successful baseline histories for the \emph{same} target and evidence permissions. If every $\pi\in\mathcal H_F$ can be simulated by $T(\pi)\in\mathcal H_B$ with $C_B(T(\pi))\le C_F(\pi)$, then $\inf_{\rho\in\mathcal H_B}C_B(\rho)\le \inf_{\pi\in\mathcal H_F}C_F(\pi)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}For each successful $\pi$, the baseline infimum is bounded above by $C_B(T(\pi))\le C_F(\pi)$; take the infimum over such $\pi$.\end{proof}
The converse claim that five members must be faster is false without extra restrictions. Parallel processing may improve \emph{wall-clock latency} while using more total resources; duplicate verification and communication may outweigh gains; or distributed views may be beneficial when a baseline cannot cheaply construct the same index. These are different optimization objectives. No positive experimental cost comparison is asserted here.
\begin{example}[A reproducible symbolic cost illustration, not a benchmark]
For this \emph{declared toy tariff}, the charge on one local service turn is $c=1+2I+J$, where $I=1$ exactly when that turn accepts a new packet into an eligible BANK and $J=1$ exactly when that turn performs a successful substantive witness/checker validation; otherwise the corresponding indicator is zero. The existence construction assigns one accepted and checked packet to each of the first $r_i$ distinct internal players, one further Knowledge no-op, and one further accepted/checked Awareness version packet. Thus there are $r_i+2$ turns, $r_i+1$ checked new packets, and one no-op. Under this tariff the total is
\[
C_i^{\rm toy}=4(r_i+1)+1=4r_i+5.
\]
For the smallest case $r_i=2$, the exact trace is:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc c@{}}\toprule
Local turn & $I$ & $J$ & $c=1+2I+J$ & New packet\\\midrule
Knowledge: $q_i$ & 1 & 1 & 4 & $q_i$\\
Awareness: historical receipt & 1 & 1 & 4 & $\mathrm{Held}_{i,t_0}(p_i)$\\
Knowledge: no-op & 0 & 0 & 1 & none\\
Awareness: version receipt & 1 & 1 & 4 & $\mathrm{VerifierVersion}_{i,t_0}(v_i)$\\\midrule
Total & 3 & 3 & 13 & 3\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The five local prefixes therefore cost $\sum_i(4r_i+5)=4\sum_i r_i+25$. When all $r_i=2$, there are $20$ local service turns, $15$ new packets and $65$ toy cost units. With five intermember transmissions of cost $\tau$ each, five receiver checks of cost $\nu$ each, and a joint target check/deposit cost $\gamma$, the federation total becomes $65+5\tau+5\nu+\gamma+C_{\Omega}$, where $C_{\Omega}\ge0$ covers separately charged federation scheduling and any further omitted operations. Here these costs are symbolic; they are not runtime measurements. The tariff presupposes that each positive deposit and its successful verification occur in the same charged turn; a different accounting convention changes the total. Shared background evidence must not be counted as a free new resource in comparisons.
\end{example}

\section{Histories, information limits, and compression}
\begin{proposition}[Federation frozen-view indistinguishability]
If two external worlds induce identical authorized federation snapshots, message-access views, initial copied state, and transition/randomness laws, then the finite dream-output distributions coincide.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Couple runs with the same random choices. Identical initial visible state and rules yield identical next-step output distributions. Induct over the number of legal transitions.\end{proof}
Thus five ATPs cannot recover an event that none of their authorized evidence records distinguish. Five histories may jointly distinguish facts invisible to each individual history; this is a genuine \emph{distributed observability} advantage, but only for differences represented in their combined accessible evidence. Historical compression must preserve the specific queries and verification receipts of interest. A quotient that merges two distinguishable historical states cannot be justified as preserving every future awareness judgment. The earlier singleton idea of safe compression can therefore be indexed by member and query class; a federated quotient needs interoperability and lifting of each cross-member certificate, not just preservation of local object-theory output.
\begin{proposition}[Sufficient condition for safe dream-history quotient]
Let $q$ map original reachable federation-copy states to quotient states. If $q$ preserves enabled legal actions, typed evidence views relevant to all admitted claims, checker decisions, BANK novelty equivalence, and exact transition costs, and every abstract legal step lifts to a matching original step and vice versa, then corresponding finite dream histories preserve accepted surpluses and charged costs.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Match the initial states and induct along the bidirectional step correspondence. Each matched step has the same checker outcome, deposit type and charge; accumulate invariants.\end{proof}
This is a sufficient structural criterion, not an assertion that a useful nontrivial quotient always exists. It also does not establish actual compression ratios or costs of constructing a quotient.

\section{The singleton theory recovered, and what cannot be recovered}
Restrict the federation game to one member $M_i$, forbid all cross-member transports, remove federation-wide settlement actions, and require the local interface to be identical to the prior singleton game (including provenance, admissibility and exact cost). Under this explicit isomorphism, the original singleton closure, noninterference, awareness and conditional reflective-ascent theorems recur. However, a general five-member execution need not factor into five independent singleton executions: communication, shared constraints, arbitration, target checking and scheduling costs couple them. In particular, federated Gestalt dependency has no nontrivial singleton counterpart, and imported object knowledge invalidates \emph{strict local} conservation while respecting the shared-theory theorem.
\begin{theorem}[Sufficient factorization under absent interaction]
If the five-member transition system is the asynchronous product of its five local transition systems and the federation scheduler merely interleaves independently legal moves with no messages, shared state updates, or extra charges, every finite global history projects to five local histories and is recoverable from those projections plus its interleaving sequence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Each global transition affects one local component and has no cross-component dependency. Project to that component; conversely replay the labeled interleaving of local transitions to reconstruct the global path.\end{proof}
Interaction can prevent factorization, although particular communicating systems may still admit special factorizations. No biconditional is claimed.


\section{Certified textbook reading as an auxiliary settlement interface}
The preceding sections analyze a single AMLD+0 conjecture settler with five ATP hosts. We now add a \emph{specified auxiliary} study interface to this constructed model. This is a formal extension proposal, \emph{not} a claim that the original AMLD+0 implementation has been changed or already contains it. The primary AMLD+0 operation remains conjecture settlement; reading and dreaming are preparatory actions that may supply certified candidate statements and internally generated targets for later settlement.
The intended control loop is simple: authorized reading acquires candidate source material, verification filters what can enter the certified archive, dreaming revisits the frozen certified history, and any internally generated target must be frozen before later settlement.
\begin{definition}[Reading packet and admission]
A textbook reading packet is\[b=(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{source},\mathrm{version},\varphi,\mathrm{kind},\Gamma_b,w,\mathrm{deps},\mathrm{scope}),\]where $\mathrm{kind}$ distinguishes an asserted proposition, a definition, a purported derivation, and an explicitly proposed axiom. A legal \emph{reading} action copies text into an untrusted candidate archive. It admits $\varphi$ as an object theorem only if a declared checker validates $w$ relative to authorized premises $\Gamma_b$ and the cross-theory translation; it admits a new independent premise only by a separate, explicitly authorized \emph{theory-extension} operation. Reading metadata alone is never proof evidence. Every action records source identity, provenance, verifier decision and nonnegative cost.
\end{definition}
Let $\Sigma_e$ be the authorized theory at study epoch $e$, and let $\widehat K_e$ be deposited certified statements. A \emph{dream} at epoch $e$ reads an authenticated immutable checkpoint $S_e$ and admits only consequences of $\Sigma_e$; a \emph{reading extension} may authorize $\Sigma_{e+1}\supseteq\Sigma_e$. The epoch must advance when the frozen axiom set changes, and earlier records must retain their original epoch and dependencies. An imported textbook statement is not automatically a theorem of $\Sigma_e$.

\begin{theorem}[Certified reading is nonamplifying without authorization]
If every admitted object statement read during epoch $e$ carries a checked derivation from $\Sigma_e$ and every dream deposit is checked from the same base, then the combined reading-and-dreaming deposits $K_e^{\mathrm{new}}$ satisfy
\[\Cn_F(\Sigma_e\cup K_e^{\mathrm{new}})=\Cn_F(\Sigma_e).\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Checker-gated acceptance gives $K_e^{\mathrm{new}}\subseteq\Cn_F(\Sigma_e)$. Apply extensivity, monotonicity and idempotence of $\Cn_F$.\end{proof}
\begin{proposition}[Nonconservative reading requires a declared change of basis or rules]
Keep the same consequence operator $\Cn_F$ throughout an epoch. Under the verification-only admission rule of Theorem~13.2, reading and dreaming cannot enlarge $\Cn_F(\Sigma_e)$. In contrast, there exists an explicitly authorized independent-premise extension satisfying $\Cn_F(\Sigma_{e+1})\ne\Cn_F(\Sigma_e)$: in classical propositional logic take $\Sigma_e=\varnothing$ and $\Sigma_{e+1}=\{p\}$. Authorization alone does not guarantee strict enlargement, and accepting a new premise is not evidence that a textbook assertion is semantically true.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}The impossibility claim follows from Theorem~13.2. For the example, $p\notin\Cn_F(\varnothing)$ but $p\in\Cn_F(\{p\})$; the basis extension, not a conservative dream, changes the closure. A change of rules/translation is a different declared theory change not covered by the fixed-operator claim.\end{proof}

\section{Proof-obligation discovery and internally frozen targets}
\begin{definition}[Template-relative undeposited subgoal and target freezing]
A \emph{template-relative undeposited subgoal} is a typed triple $(T,L,d)$, with $d$ a checker-accepted conditional derivation $\Sigma_e\cup\{L\}\vdash_F T$ and no accepted proof packet for $L$ in a declared, exhaustively searchable \emph{finite} archive at the observation epoch. This shows that $L$ would suffice to complete the chosen derivation route. It establishes neither $\Sigma_e\nvdash_F L$ nor that \emph{every} proof of $T$ must use $L$, nor that $T$ is not already provable. A claim that $L$ is indispensable across every permitted proof strategy would require a separate argument ruling out all allowed alternative routes. Even $\Sigma_e\nvdash_F T$ would not by itself show that every successful extended proof must use this particular $L$; an archive lookup establishes neither statement. An internal target is a tuple $\tau=(\mathrm{id},T,\Sigma_e,\mathrm{rules},V,\mathrm{budget},\mathrm{epoch})$, frozen \emph{before} its own settlement attempt. Its creation and scheduling require separate authorization and may not replace an externally imposed target. A certificate checked with independent premises admitted only at epoch $e+1$ is never a settlement of the older tuple at epoch $e$.
\end{definition}
\begin{theorem}[Constructive recognition of a template-relative open subgoal]
There exists a finite five-member study state in which dreaming soundly identifies a useful-for-a-specified-template but undeposited intermediate formula and selects a corresponding reading query, without asserting necessity, unprovability, or knowledge of unread sources.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}Take distinct propositional atoms $p,q,T$ and freeze $\Sigma_e=\{p,(p\land q)\to T\}$, each member of this basis having an explicit authorized premise receipt. The accepted archive contains a checked certificate for $p$ but no accepted packet proving $q$; its finite authenticated index is exhaustive for deposited packets. From $\Sigma_e\cup\{q\}$ the checker constructs $p\land q$ and then $T$ using the \emph{certified basis implication} $(p\land q)\to T$. The exact archive query establishes only absence of a deposited $q$ certificate. The Awareness process records this bounded fact, Knowledge emits ``find an independently checkable derivation of $q$ or methods relevant to $q$,'' and an authorized scheduler may select the query. In this witness $q$ is useful along that template, but nothing about an unread book's contents, universal proof necessity, or undecidability has been inferred.\end{proof}

\section{A constructive self-directed reading--dreaming--settlement cycle}
\begin{definition}[Staged study execution and epoch discipline]
A complete study stage $e$ comprises, in order, a legal reading action $R_e$, a certified dream episode $D_e$, a newly constructed and frozen internal target $\tau_e$, and a verifier-gated settlement attempt $S_e$. \emph{Accepted study success} means that the independent terminal gate accepted a certificate matching precisely the target's frozen formula, authorized premise basis, rules, verifier and epoch; an ordinary BANK acceptance is insufficient. Each stage has a distinct study-stage identifier, and every change to the authorized axiom basis also creates a fresh \emph{basis epoch}; distinct stage identifiers need not imply different axiom sets. Explicit basis transitions happen between stages (or require a fresh target before settlement). All local, transport, storage, reading, authorization, and settlement costs are recorded. The stage may settle an already provable formula: novel target identifiers do not imply novel logical consequences.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Arbitrarily long certified self-directed study]
For every $N\ge1$ there exists a finite set-coded, five-ATP, AMLD+0-compatible auxiliary study model with $N$ stages, no externally assigned conjectures during those stages, and $N$ distinct internally generated targets with accepted proof certificates. Every target is frozen before its attempt; every source and dream deposit is verifier-gated; no accepted statement rests on an unverified textbook assertion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use propositional atoms $p_0,\ldots,p_N$ and the fixed authorized axiom/rule corpus $\Sigma=\{p_0\}\cup\{p_e\to p_{e+1}:0\le e<N\}$, accompanied by explicit premise receipts. At stage $e$, a legal reading interface selects the already authorized textbook packet for $p_e\to p_{e+1}$ and verifies its premise status; the current archive contains a certificate for $p_e$ (initially the $p_0$ premise). A dream replays the authenticated $p_e$ certificate, checks modus ponens and proposes $p_{e+1}$. The authorized internal-target constructor freezes $\tau_e$ for $T_e=p_{e+1}$ under the same $\Sigma$. The Proof specialist supplies the finite derivation and the terminal verifier accepts it. The other four specialists and internal players may take legal charged no-ops on a declared nested cyclic schedule; no role is reassigned. Each study-stage index is fresh even though the fixed theory $\Sigma$ is unchanged; no basis epoch change is implied. All instructions are finite and set-coded, and induction on $e$ gives $N$ accepted targets. Source readings only retrieve declared axioms; the episode supplies new \emph{deposited} consequences, not new consequences beyond $\Cn_F(\Sigma)$. The entire curriculum is constructed in advance: this proves existence of arbitrarily many certified stages, not autonomous selection of unknown useful textbooks.\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[An infinite constructive existence model]
There exists a countably encoded, effectively presented study corpus and a fair infinite execution with infinitely many different certified internal proof targets, provided there is no global finite budget.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}Take $\Sigma=\{p_0\}\cup\{p_e\to p_{e+1}:e\in\mathbb N\}$ with a computable rule enumerator; apply the stage construction for each $e$. Choose a dovetailed cyclic federation schedule that services all five members and each of their finitely many internal processes infinitely often. Each individual stage takes finitely many steps and has a finite checked certificate. The resulting infinite execution is a mathematical construction; it is not an observed infinite run or a finite-time completeness guarantee.\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A witnessed influence of dreaming on later reading choice]
There exists a complete two-stage, five-member auxiliary study execution in which a checked dream at stage~$0$ selects the next reading query, an authorized basis extension and checked reading at stage~$1$ supply a previously undeposited formula, and an independently checked certificate settles a \emph{newly frozen} target under the new basis. The older frozen target is never retroactively settled using the extension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Work in classical propositional logic with distinct atoms $p,q,r,T$. Initially freeze $\Sigma_0=\{p,(p\land q)\to T\}$, with signed/authenticated \emph{and logically checked} premise receipts, and let the accepted finite archive contain a proof packet for $p$ but none for $q$. Put an indexed corpus packet $b_1$ containing the two proposed independent premises $r,r\to q$ and a syntactically checkable derivation $r,r\to q\vdash q$. Importantly, the existence of $b_1$ does not make $q$ a theorem of $\Sigma_0$.

\emph{Stage 0 (basis epoch 0).} $R_0$ is a legal reading action that retrieves and checks the already authorized implication $(p\land q)\to T$ as a basis packet. $D_0$ checks the conditional route $\Sigma_0,q\vdash T$, performs an exact exhaustive deposited-packet lookup for $q$, and emits the query $Q(q)$; this is a certified dream episode but does \emph{not} deposit $q$. An authorized constructor then freezes $\tau_0=(\mathrm{id}_0,p,\Sigma_0,\mathcal R,V_0,B_0,0)$, and $S_0$ uses the original checked premise receipt to settle $p$ at the independent terminal gate. This stage's target is intentionally elementary; its role is to make the preliminary inquiry a complete stage rather than a truncated one.

\emph{Between stages.} The trace records that $Q(q)$ selects corpus packet $b_1$. Only after an explicitly separate admission decision authorizes $\Delta=\{r,r\to q\}$ do we freeze the new basis $\Sigma_1=\Sigma_0\cup\Delta$, enter basis epoch 1, and charge the extension. The constructor does not modify $\tau_0$.

\emph{Stage 1 (basis epoch 1).} $R_1$ reads $b_1$, checks the exact $\Delta$-premise dependencies, and validates its proof of $q$ against $\Sigma_1$; no $q$ theorem is attributed to $\Sigma_0$. $D_1$ uses the checked $q$ receipt and $p,(p\land q)\to T$ to build a certified derivation of $T$ under $\Sigma_1$. The controller now creates and freezes the \emph{different} target $\tau_1=(\mathrm{id}_1,T,\Sigma_1,\mathcal R,V_1,B_1,1)$. Finally, $S_1$ presents the complete derivation with its authorized premise ledger to the independent terminal verifier, which accepts exactly this $\tau_1$.

All five members and their internal processes can be interleaved by the persistent-enabledness schedule of Theorem~9.1; idle specialists use charged no-ops. The successful path is constructed, not asserted to have occurred in an unmodified AMLD+0 engine. The conditional dependency $Q(q)\mapsto b_1$ makes the second reading choice depend on the first dream. A model satisfying $p$ and $(p\land q)\to T$ but falsifying $q$ confirms that the new $q$ certificate cannot be backdated to epoch~0.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[No universal gain from studying]
Neither all dream episodes nor all reading stages must produce a new accepted theorem; moreover, adding certified study actions can increase total charged cost without improving settlement success.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Take a finite theory with all eligible target theorems already deposited, or select a verified duplicate-reading packet. All novelty checks reject additional deposits. Give every reading and verification action a positive cost. The new execution adds cost but no new target outcome.\end{proof}

\section{Research conjectures left deliberately open}
The constructive theorems above resolve \emph{existential} claims within a precisely defined auxiliary model, not the following stronger assertions.
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0.6cm,style=nextline]
\item[Conjecture A (Unmodified implementation).] The currently deployed AMLD+0 already exposes all the reading, source-ingestion, target-generation, and simulation interfaces used above, so careful prompting alone can execute the entire cycle. This is \textbf{UNKNOWN} without an audited interface mapping and actual execution trace.
\item[Conjecture B (Measured settlement improvement).] On a declared benchmark distribution, self-directed study lowers fully charged expected settlement cost or raises certified settlement yield compared with matched equal-evidence baselines. This is \textbf{OPEN}; the constructions prove no empirical advantage.
\item[Conjecture C (Useful curriculum selection).] A fixed effective policy can repeatedly select relevant previously unread mathematical material for a nontrivial broad target class with a provable approximation or regret bound. This is \textbf{OPEN} absent corpus and objective assumptions.
\item[Conjecture D (Reflection-derived search power).] Additional certified historical or reflective awareness yields a demonstrable, reproducible benefit beyond equivalent nonreflective proof-history indexing under matched costs. This is \textbf{OPEN}.
\item[Conjecture E (Finite-resource success).] An unchanged five-ATP AMLD+0 system can guarantee the desired internally generated settlements within a uniform finite budget on a declared target family. This requires explicit bounded-search hypotheses; fairness alone is insufficient.
\end{description}
\noindent\textbf{Interpretive boundary.} The constructed study episodes do not constitute independently certified AMLD+0 terminal runs; proposed target generation is auxiliary and authorized rather than silently inserted into AMLD+0's native frozen-target semantics. None of the above implies autonomy, human dreaming, consciousness, proof-search optimality, or universal theorem discovery.

\section{Consequences and a disciplined research agenda}
Five ATPs alter the nature of dreaming in six ways. First, a member can gain theorems from other members without any enlargement of the global shared theory. Second, \emph{genuine} cross-member deductive dependency becomes possible, with coalition sizes from two to five. Third, the historical awareness language changes from self-reference to independently gated mutual reference and nested member/time-indexed claims. Fourth, the federation scheduler and local board-players require a two-level fairness proof, not merely a single spinner. Fifth, cost is no longer local: transportation, receiver verification, synchronization, competing member work and duplicate proofs affect whether cooperation is beneficial. Sixth, history compression becomes a multi-view preservation problem: it must retain the cross-member distinctions needed by the selected query class.

These consequences suggest precise next tasks: implement and audit a five-specialty assignment under the actual AMLD+0 protocol; choose a frozen common language or verified translator; define cryptographically and logically scoped cross-member receipt checking; benchmark coalition size $k=1,\ldots,5$ against a centralized equal-access baseline with both wall time and total charged work; test local-versus-global proof reuse; quantify when provenance-aware history quotients reduce memory without erasing valid historical queries; and investigate whether reflection constructors produce any empirically useful extra theorem-search guidance. None is claimed solved by these mathematical existence constructions. The new reading-and-dreaming extension does establish finite and infinite \emph{existence models} of self-directed study, but not the performance or implementation conjectures above.
