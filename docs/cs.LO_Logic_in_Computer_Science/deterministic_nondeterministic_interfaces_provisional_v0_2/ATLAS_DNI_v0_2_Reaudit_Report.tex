\section*{4. Independent computational checks (not proof substitutes)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Exhaustive three-state support graphs:} 343 directed graphs with all three outdegrees nonzero were enumerated ($7^3=343$). Exactly 39 satisfied the no-branching-on-cycles condition and 304 failed. In every satisfying case, length-11 path-word counts had stabilized and matched the graph recurrence. Every failing case had strictly more positive length-11 words than length-2 words. This checks many instances but is not an exhaustive verification of arbitrary state counts.
\item \textbf{Independent four-state exhaustive check:} All $15^4=50{,}625$ four-vertex directed graphs with nonempty outdegree were separately enumerated. Cycle membership was computed using positive-length transitive closure rather than the depth-first check above. Exactly 904 satisfied the graph criterion and 49,721 failed. Each satisfying graph had stabilized path-word counts by length 12; each failing graph had more length-12 words than length-4 words. This provides a second finite consistency check, not a proof of the general theorem.
    \item \textbf{Four-hidden-state example:} The rational interface with $E(a)=\tfrac13\delta_{w_1}+\tfrac23\delta_{w_2}$, $E(b)=\delta_{w_3}$, $E(c)=\delta_{w_4}$ was checked entry-by-entry for (S1) and (S2).
\item \textbf{Typesetting:} The revised TeX compiles to eight letter-size PDF pages with no overfull boxes, LaTeX errors, or undefined references in the final pass. All eight pages were rendered and reviewed; two minor underfull paragraph diagnostics do not conceal mathematics.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{5. Remaining qualifications and publication advice}
\textbf{Q1 --- Originality: NOT CERTIFIED.} The newly organized finite-state characterization is proved, but elementary path/cycle classification and automata realizations may have earlier formulations. The audit establishes mathematical correctness, not international literature priority. Present the result as a self-contained finite-classical theorem pending extended literature review.

\textbf{Q2 --- Theory-of-theories scope: LIMITED.} The paper formally states a joint-model criterion for disjoint first-order signatures; it does not construct a universal mixed-signature interface or prove conservative embedding of arbitrary input theories. A syntactic pushout need not yield semantic compatibility.

\textbf{Q3 --- Theoretical costs: QUALIFIED.} $m_*$ optimizes the number of hidden states over freely chosen deterministic $f$, readout $R$, and preparation $E$. It does not minimize bits of storage, probability-description complexity, physical implementability, runtime, or a preassigned source deterministic theory.

\textbf{Q4 --- Semantics: LIMITED.} Probability is classical and discrete time; no nonclassical measurement, contextuality, causal locality or physical reconstruction theorem is established. The role of statistical preparation remains declared, rather than suppressed.

\textbf{Q5 --- Algorithmic precision: ACCEPTABLE.} The exact recurrence is finite and testable. Bit complexity and optimization when the source structures are independently fixed remain future work.
