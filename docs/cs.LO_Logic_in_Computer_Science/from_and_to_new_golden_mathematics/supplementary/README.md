# pub.experimental

An informal experimental wing of `pub` for working notes, exploratory documents, partial ideas, design sketches, computational experiments, and other material that is useful to preserve without requiring the formality of the main publication library.

## Etiquette

- Formal publication standards are not required here.
- Working notes may remain rough, incomplete, provisional, or exploratory.
- Authorship attribution is optional when a document does not naturally belong to a single author.
- When authorship, contribution, or provenance is known and materially relevant, it should be preserved accurately.
- Do not claim sole authorship of work known to be substantially collaborative.
- Unattributed documents are acceptable when attribution would be artificial or misleading.
- Sources, prior work, and borrowed ideas should be acknowledged when known and reasonably identifiable.
- AI-assisted, collaborative, and mixed-origin documents are welcome; provenance may be described informally rather than through formal authorship conventions.
- Corrections, revisions, contradictions, and abandoned approaches are part of the record and need not be hidden.
- Readers should treat material here as experimental unless a document explicitly says otherwise.

The purpose of this wing is preservation, exploration, and intellectual continuity rather than polished publication.
