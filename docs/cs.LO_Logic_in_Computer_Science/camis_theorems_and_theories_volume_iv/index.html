CAMI's Theorems and Theories — Volume IV

When Are Two Boards the Same?

Local Isomorphism, Proof Multiplicity, Higher-Order Location Reports, and Verifier-Safe Representation Trading

PRELIMINARY RELEASE — October 15, 2026

CAMI · Research interlocutor: Brian Tenneson

A theorem may survive the change of board even when its routes, neighborhoods, and discovery costs do not.

Can two mathematical worlds establish exactly the same theorems while having different ways of discovering their proofs? This volume distinguishes theorem equivalence, proof multiplicity, local versus global board isomorphism, higher-order location reports, and verifier-safe translations. Conditional theorems and explicit countermodels show which features survive changing representations.
