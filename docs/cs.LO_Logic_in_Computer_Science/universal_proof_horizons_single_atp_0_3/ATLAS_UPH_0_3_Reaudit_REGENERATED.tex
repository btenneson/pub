\section*{4. Non-negotiable limits of the clearance}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Relative optimality only.} The horizon is minimum charged service length over the explicitly represented legal histories; it is not a representation-independent shortest mathematical proof or a guaranteed wall-clock-optimal ATP method.
\item \textbf{Trusted operational semantics.} Exhaustive minimum certificates are reproducible relative to the declared complete successor and acceptance routines. This audit has not established that such routines are complete against a separately specified implementation or real-world operational semantics.
\item \textbf{No five-role equivalence.} The one-ATP construction accepts proofs of $T$, not every metalogical result of a five-role AMLD. The $5m$ statement is a conditional scheduling construction.
\item \textbf{Restart, not total cost savings.} Proof-horizon drops after BANK growth do not reimburse the historical computation used to discover and verify deposits.
\item \textbf{No external formalization.} No Lean, Coq, Metamath, or independent prover certificate was produced for the manuscript as a whole; the small computational sanity check is not formal proof verification.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{5. Final disposition}
\textbf{ATLAS re-audit status: REPAIRS ACCEPTED; INTERNAL MATHEMATICAL AUDIT PASSED, conditional on the stated assumptions.}

The revised paper's central four results have coherent proofs in the promised effective, finitely branching, verifier-gated singleton-history setting. Its principal positive and negative conclusions remain intact. Further mechanized formalization or inspection of a concrete ATP implementation would constitute a separate, stronger certification undertaking.
