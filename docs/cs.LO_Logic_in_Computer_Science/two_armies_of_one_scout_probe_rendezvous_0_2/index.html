Two Armies of One: Certified Scout–Probe Rendezvous in Proof Search

Brian Tenneson · Logic in Computer Science · ATLAS-repaired preliminary release, version 0.2

“There are two armies of one: one Scout and one Probe. Their meeting point need not be specified in advance.”

Version 0.2 · p. 2

The Scout works backward from a target using verified conditional proof routes, while the Probe works forward from hypotheses. The article proves conditional certificate rendezvous, not automatic useful early meeting or speedup.
