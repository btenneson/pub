\section*{3. Independent checks of the principal mathematics}
Set $H(u)=\sum_jW_j(1-e^{-k_ju_j})$, with $W_j\ge0$, $k_j>0$, and $F(u)=1-e^{-H(u)}$. Its Hessian obeys
\[
v^\top\nabla^2F(u)v=-e^{-H(u)}\left[\sum_j W_jk_j^2e^{-k_ju_j}v_j^2+\bigl(v^\top\nabla H(u)\bigr)^2\right]\le0.
\]
This analytic identity proves concavity, independently of the probability interpretation. Existence follows by continuity/compactness; strict concavity holds on the feasible set when distinct feasible allocations cannot differ only along zero-weight coordinates. Stationarity of the equivalent objective $H$ gives $W_jk_je^{-k_ju_j}=\lambda c_j$ for positive-weight interior channels; the lower-bound clip and budget multiplier are correct.

\textbf{New quantization result.} On a static feasible region with entrywise nonnegative capacity matrix $A$ and $c_j>0$, rounding $u_j^*$ down to $m_j+q_j\lfloor(u_j^*-m_j)/q_j\rfloor$ preserves feasibility, because the resource inequalities are monotone. Since $0\le\partial_jF\le W_jk_j$, the objective loss is no more than $\sum_j W_jk_jq_j$. This is a genuine mathematical addition to the repaired manuscript, but is not a general rounding theorem for arbitrary $A$ or an adaptive scheduler guarantee.

\textbf{Finite reproducibility checks (not AMLD experiments).} An accompanying deterministic script checked all 8 evidence triples; 5,000 random Hessians and finite-difference gradients (largest numerical Hessian eigenvalue $1.7\times10^{-16}$, consistent with roundoff); 3,000 event-share conservation examples; 4,000 monotone-feasibility rounding instances; 4,000 finite-set $2\varepsilon$ comparisons; and 1,000 contradiction-Hessian examples. All checks passed. The mathematical proofs, not this sampling, establish the universal statements.

\section*{4. What the repairs do not settle}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{R\arabic*.},leftmargin=2.6em,itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Calibration and predictive validity: OPEN.} The map $\mathcal K_x$ has not been fitted and independently evaluated on predeclared held-out AMLD tasks. The optimized value is a valid \emph{surrogate} regardless of empirical fit, not an established success rate.
\item \textbf{Joint event law and deduplication implementation: OPEN.} Credit shares conserve attribution in the declared ledger. They do not prove independent opportunity arrival streams, handle every correlated reuse phenomenon, or implement canonical event equivalence in an actual system.
\item \textbf{Class-specific probabilities: NOT CLAIMED.} Only aggregate terminal settlement probability is conditionally modeled. A sound joint model for $P,R,I,K$ needs an additional event structure and consistency constraints. In particular known-consistent sound $T$ precludes genuine $K$ outcomes.
\item \textbf{Actual discrete/adaptive performance: OPEN.} The static quantization theorem does not include policy-dependent future states, random verifier/communication overhead, early stopping, or coefficient refitting. Separate approved experimentation would be needed.
\item \textbf{Historical-source identity: NOT VERIFIED.} The original six-page predecessor was not part of the supplied regenerated ZIP; preservation of the predecessor's \emph{historical text} cannot be independently certified. The regenerated ZIP itself and its original 19-section order are the reproducible comparison baseline.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{5. Final release recommendation}
\textbf{ATLAS recommendation: PASS for a carefully labeled conditional mathematical modeling paper.} The prior audit's four high-priority clarification gaps have been repaired \emph{at the manuscript level}: probability is expressly conditional, accounting is auditable, class-specific claims are withheld, and static-to-discrete scope is delimited with a theorem.
