Architecture and expository article · cs.AI — Artificial Intelligence

DATA MIND 3.0 presents a modular architecture for difficult mathematical search that separates formal mathematical authority from heuristic guidance, learning, speculation, resource allocation, and operational control. It explains the Verifier/BANK/FUTUREBANK trust boundary, installation and module contracts, safe upgrades, bounded Scouts, resource accounting, search-geometry transformation, and worked difficult-case workflows.
