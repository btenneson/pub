Track B — NSA & Presentation Speedups

How can theoremhood remain fixed while proof-search cost changes, and what does nonstandard analysis contribute when passing from unlimited derivability to bounded settlement?

B1. Trading lens: theoremhood versus budget

Concept checkpoint

Before reading the NSA paper, keep two levels separate. The unbounded question is whether the target follows at all. The bounded question is whether a verifier-acceptable route can be found under a declared proof-length, expansion, time, or memory budget.

Read with this test: every claimed “speedup” should identify the cost model, account for translation/certification overhead, and distinguish proof-length effects from controller/search effects.

B3. Proof-density-after-trading checkpoint

Concept checkpoint · interaction effects matter

Do not assume a trade that shortens one route also raises local proof density or improves a particular controller. Horizon, density, branching, translation cost, and settlement probability may move in different directions.

Be able to ask: Does Trading help alone? Does it make QH stronger or weaker? Does BANK compensate for a bad interaction? Is the apparent speedup reproducible across seeds and theorems?

Conjecture Laboratory

These are discussion targets, not a list of established theorems. The label on each card tells the reader what kind of claim is still open.

Status discipline: “Mathematical conjecture” asks for proof or counterexample. “Architectural hypothesis” asks whether a design property follows under stated system assumptions. “Experimental hypothesis” requires preregistered empirical evidence. Negative results are informative.
Mathematical conjecture

Finite-budget divergence under consequence equivalence

Characterize when two consequence-equivalent presentations can have sharply different theorem sets reachable below the same proof-length or resource budget, and how large the required budget rescaling can become.

Mathematical / proof complexity

Which trades preserve efficient proof translation?

Identify useful classes of certified trades with polynomial or otherwise controlled proof translation in both directions, rather than mere equality of consequence closure.

Mathematical conjecture

Trade-then-quotient exposure

When can an invariant or quotient obstruction that is opaque in one presentation become structurally obvious in a consequence-equivalent presentation, with a sound transport back to the original settlement question?

Experimental hypothesis

QH + BANK synergy

Does verifier-gated BANK reuse improve Quotient Hunter directly when Trading is disabled, or did the earlier full T+Q+B speed advantage merely compensate for a negative Trading×QH interaction?

Experimental hypothesis

Adaptive presentation switching

Can a controller reliably learn when the expected downstream value of changing presentation exceeds the generation, certification, translation, and verification cost of the switch?

Architectural hypothesis

Federated BANK without semantic drift

Under what dependency-visibility and provenance conditions can verified items move among CORE, LOCAL, or COUPLED memory nodes while final theoremhood remains independent of storage topology?

Experimental hypothesis

Does federation improve settlement?

Even if federation preserves correctness, does selective memory placement reduce irrelevant retrievals, resource cost, or cross-domain interference enough to improve held-out settlement?

Mathematical / reflective

Bounded self-access versus unbounded self-theory

Can certified presentation changes lower the budget needed to reach already-provable self-descriptive statements while leaving the machine’s unbounded extensional self-theory unchanged?

Safety / verification

Verifier invariance under aggressive search transformation

How far can Trading, quotient pruning, BANK macros, and learned control be pushed while maintaining the invariant that every final settlement still compiles to evidence accepted by the unchanged trust-anchor verifier?

Experimental hypothesis

One canonical presentation or a portfolio?

For a theorem family, is there a presentation that generalizes well across held-out targets, or is a budgeted portfolio of equivalent presentations intrinsically more robust?
