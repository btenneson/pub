What this companion does

The story asks what the second “I know” adds. This certificate gives the technical answer: the second knowledge operator is not free introspection. It is obtained through a verifier-facing receipt record, an accepted meta-record, and an explicit one-level epistemic-domain premise.

Status: conditional verifier-facing proof skeleton. It is not yet executable until the verifier predicates and certificate rules are frozen in the verifier language.

Settlement target

τA(t) := KA,t+1(KA,t AMLD(⟨A⟩))

Requested terminal settlement: P. Trusted checker: V.

Proof route in one paragraph

First, the system certifies the structural AMLD claim σA := AMLD(⟨A⟩). It deposits an accepted record r0 witnessing that claim, yielding KA,tσA. Then it constructs a receipt certificate ρ(r0) whose claim is the definitional expansion of KA,tσA. After that receipt is accepted as meta-record r1, the system uses r1 as the witness for KA,t+1(KA,tσA).

Firewall

VC001 does not claim that each component ATP is epistemically aware or unaware. Their ATP status is structural input only. The sole epistemic subject is the federation A.
