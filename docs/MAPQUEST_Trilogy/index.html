Series climax

MAPQUEST Target-Certification Theorem

Under the stated verifier-gating, finite-provenance, and soundness assumptions, if a finite MAPQUEST run deposits the fixed target T in the trusted BANK, its recorded dependency DAG can be extracted as a finite verifier-accepted certificate.

T ∈ Bm  ⇒  V(Γ,T,ΠT) = 1  ⇒  Γ ⊢ T.

The navigation machinery does not prove T merely by reaching a favorable coordinate. It proves T when the route it constructs consists of legal verifier-accepted inferences whose terminal formula is exactly T.
