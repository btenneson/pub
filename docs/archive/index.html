The Universal Proof-Horizon Operator — From MIU and the nth Well-Formed Formula to ZFC, Predator, and Automated Logical Deciders

Core paper · cs.LO — Logic in Computer Science

A research monograph connecting MIU, effective Gödel-coded enumeration, minimum-cost proof search, the SIC Proof-Horizon framework, theorem-space growth, Predator, and automated logical deciders. Its central theorem identifies a partial computable operator that returns a canonical minimum-cost proof for an enumerated theorem under a fixed effective proof calculus and an effectively proper computable cost; on non-theorems it may diverge. The manuscript explicitly does not claim to decide ZFC or evade Church-style undecidability or Gödel–Rosser incompleteness, and treats proof navigation, verification, and logical status as distinct problems.

UNCUT · UNAUDITED · PREVIEW EDITION — Early public research release. Presented without a fresh independent audit or editorial cut; claims, citations, and scope should be checked against the manuscript and sources before relying on them.
