# From Search Dynamics to Semi-Ideal Computers — Version 0.3

**Discovery Addendum — Fully Integrated Edition**

This directory contains the LaTeX source and compiled PDF for Version 0.3 of *From Search Dynamics to Semi-Ideal Computers: Cumulative Verified Histories, Rationally Weighted Theorem Mazes, Reflective ATPs, and Discovery* by Brian Tenneson.

Version 0.3 preserves the maze-first, verifier-backed SIC/ATP/ALD/AMLD framework and the Control AMLD experimental section from the prior edition. It adds a fully integrated Section 19, **Discovery**, and moves the conclusion to Section 20.

The new section distinguishes:

- **weak discovery**: verifier-accepted novelty modulo a prospectively declared verifier-respecting equivalence;
- **operational discovery**: weak discovery that measurably improves finite-budget settlement cost or verified reach;
- **discovery hazard**: finite-horizon probability of discovery conditional on the current state;
- **discovery depth**: machine- and metric-relative cost of reaching a discovery class;
- **discovery gain**: controlled change in discovery probability produced by an intervention;
- **forward-chaining rung evaluation**: train on earlier audited rungs, freeze the policy, then expose the next sealed rung;
- **motif-semigroup search**: structured proof variation inspired by musical development rather than fixed-probability noise.

The search funnel is correspondingly extended with discovery-sensitive control after exact/learned navigation and before certificate-bearing fixed points. A new Discovery-transfer conjecture is included with the research conjectures.

The PDF filename contains underscores and no spaces for repository portability. The title printed inside the PDF uses normal spaces.
